<br>

Nature's Feast adds unique foods that fit the Let's Do style with ingredients from Naturalist mod.  
<br>
This mod also adds 3 new crops which you can find in different biomes while exploring.  
There's a new "Bird Cage" block that lets you capture birds and showcase them in your house.  
<br>
Let's jump into a world and start cooking!

<br><br>

## New Ingredients

- New Meats
- Chili Pepper
- Garlic
- Black Pepper

All these ingredients can be processed with tools in Let's Do mods.

<br>

## Bird Cage

- Craft a bird cage with 1 iron ingot, glass, and any type of planks
- *Right Click* on a bird to capture it and *Shift Right Click* to release it
- *Shift Right Click* on a placed bird cage to pick it up

Find beautiful birds and keep them in your house!
